ThumbprintRead straight from Solana

Check before you buy.

Paste a token or contract address. This page asks Solana whether an account's owner approved it with their fingerprint, and whether that account has since been reported as hacked.

No Thumbprint server is involved. Your browser reads the records from a public Solana node, then runs the same check a wallet or another program can put in front of a swap: if the answer is no, the whole transaction fails.
