Your account can be hacked. Your identity can't.

Crypto accounts on X approve every post with a fingerprint on a Solana Seeker. When someone takes over the account, their posts show up as not signed, and the drainer link is blocked.

A password can be stolen. A thumb on a phone cannot.

Project and founder accounts get taken over, and the attacker posts a "claim your airdrop" link to followers who trust the account. Thumbprint ties each post to a device the attacker does not have.

01 · WRITE

Post like you always do

A small button sits next to Post in X's own composer. Press it and the draft goes to your Seeker. Nothing else about X changes.

02 · APPROVE

Read it on your phone, then use your fingerprint

The Seeker shows the words you are about to publish. Its Seed Vault signs them only after a double tap and a fingerprint, so nothing on your laptop can approve a post for you.

03 · READ

Followers see the mark

Approved posts carry a fingerprint next to the name, the same size as the verified check. The card shows when it was approved, on which device, and links to the record on Solana.

04 · HIJACKED

A post without the phone stands out

If an account that signs its posts publishes one that is not approved, it is flagged in red and any links in it are blocked until the reader chooses to open them.

05 · KILL SWITCH

One tap warns everyone

The owner taps "My account was hacked" on the Seeker. Every reader sees the warning on the profile right away, even while the attacker still holds the X session.

What the chain does

The crypto stays out of sight. Readers install an extension. Owners use a phone they already carry, and never pay a fee.

Words, signed and checked onchain

The Seeker signs a readable message. The program verifies that signature itself and computes the post's hash from the signed text, so the server can pay fees but cannot forge an approval.

One device, one account

Each Seeker carries a Genesis Token minted once per device. The device's record is derived from it, and a handle can be linked only once, after the owner proves the X account is theirs.

Public and permanent

Every approval is an event anyone can read. A QuickNode Stream carries them to the index the extension looks up, and each mark links to its transaction.

Try it

Reading needs only the extension. Signing needs a Solana Seeker.

Readers

  1. Download the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the extension folder.
  4. Open X. Signed posts carry the mark.

Account owners

  1. Install the extension and open its popup.
  2. Scan the pairing code with your Seeker.
  3. Prove the X account is yours, then link it with a fingerprint.
  4. Press the Thumbprint button before you post.
