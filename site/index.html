        I post on X
        
Protect your followers

        
You need a Solana Seeker. After a one-time setup, every post takes one touch of your finger.

        
  1. Install the app on your Seeker.
  2. Link your X account. You do this once.
  3. Post from the app or your laptop, and approve with your fingerprint.

        Get the Seeker app 
      

    

  


  

    

      

        
01Write
Post like you always do
A small button sits next to Post in X's own composer. Press it and the draft goes to your Seeker. Nothing else about X changes.

        
      

      

        
02Approve
Read it on your phone, then use your fingerprint
The Seeker shows the words you are about to publish. A key locked inside the phone's secure hardware signs them, and it works only with your fingerprint. No password or PIN can stand in, so nothing on your laptop and no stolen login can approve a post for you. You can also write the post right in the app.

        

      

      

        
03Read
Followers see the mark
Approved posts carry a fingerprint next to the name, the same size as the verified check. The card shows when it was approved, on which device, and links to the record on Solana.

        
      

      

        
04Addresses
The real token address, and only the real one
When an approved post names a Solana address, that address is recorded on Solana as official for the account. Readers see it marked green in anyone's post. A different address from the same account gets a red warning, and any wallet can run the same check before you buy.

        
      

      

        
05Hijacked
A post without the phone stands out
If an account that signs its posts publishes one that is not approved, it is flagged in red and any links in it are blocked until the reader chooses to open them.

        
      

      

        
06Kill switch
One tap warns everyone
The owner taps "My account was hacked" on the Seeker. Every reader sees the warning on the profile right away, even while the attacker still holds the X session.

        
      

    

  


  

    

      

        
01Write
Post from the app
Type your post, touch the sensor, and X opens with it ready to send. What you see on this screen is exactly what your fingerprint approves.

        
02Approve
Or post from your laptop, approve on the phone
Write on X like normal and press the Thumbprint button. The post shows up on your phone. Touch the sensor and it goes out. Reject it and it doesn't.

        
03Hacked
Lost your account? Let everyone know
A hacker can take your password. They can't take your phone. One touch puts a warning on your profile that every reader sees, even while the hacker is still logged in.

      

      

    

  

  


  

    
Why you can trust the green mark.
You don't have to trust X, and you don't have to trust us. Every approval is checked and stored on Solana, where anyone can look.

    

      
01
It can't be faked
Your phone signs the exact words of your post. Solana checks that signature before it records anything. Change one letter and it no longer matches.

      
02
One phone, one account
Every Seeker is born with a one-of-a-kind token. Your account is tied to your phone and nobody else's, so there can't be a second "you".

      
03
Wallets can check too
Every record is public. Before you buy, any wallet or app can ask Solana "did this account's phone approve this address?" No login, no asking us.
